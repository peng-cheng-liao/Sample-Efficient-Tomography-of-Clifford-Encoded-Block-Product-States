  - the generic_d_ge_2 execution branch;
  - peeling, recovery, grouping, syndrome/localization, and tomography stages;
  - five-stage fixed-budget allocations, including a positive grouping cap;
  - recovered-block tomography with register size bounded by d;

change candidate generation, budgets, feasibility, estimation, or ranking.
